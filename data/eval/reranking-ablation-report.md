# Document Re-Ranking Ablation Report

Generated by evaluate.py's `run_reranking_ablation` against 101 ground-truth question(s) (data/eval/ground_truth_filtered.json). method=hybrid, rrf_k=10 (recorded default) for all three arms, top_k=10. Evaluates the existing `_boost_by_country` country-metadata re-rank in `src/retrieval/search.py` as the rubric's document re-ranking best-practice point.

**Metadata coverage check:** 3783/3783 (100.0%) indexed chunks carry a non-empty `countries` field. This is the real regression risk for a re-rank that is a total reorder, not a weighted one — a relevant chunk missing `countries` would be demoted below every tagged chunk and could fall out of top_k. At this coverage level that risk is negligible (every chunk carries the field).

**Provenance / circularity check:** the ground-truth set (`ground_truth_filtered.json`, mechanically filtered from a full re-run of `ground_truth.py`) was generated and manually circularity-reviewed entirely on 2026-07-22, one day *before* the P2 country-metadata boost was added to `search.py` (commit `756b28e`, 2026-07-23). `ground_truth.py` also never calls `search()` itself — questions are generated straight from sampled chunk text. So this ground truth was not created with knowledge of the boost's existence; no circularity risk from this ablation against this specific ground-truth set.

**Bit-identical assertion:** arms (b) pool-only and (c) current were asserted programmatically identical on every non-firing question (the boost is definitionally a no-op there) before this report was written — passed, no mismatches.
